# Schedule — Two Clear Nights

One night lost to weather. Same shape as the three-night plan — **moose drive → lake → core →
cabin for meteors** — just with the creative session folded into the tail of night two instead
of getting its own evening.

Read [PRIORITIES.md](PRIORITIES.md) first.

## What changes from three nights

- **The Veil gets dropped.** The refractor's block goes to Dark Shark both nights so it
  accumulates ~6 hours instead of two half-finished targets.
- **Creative work moves to night 2's 11:05–11:25 slot**, sharing the window with the last of the
  core frames rather than having a session to itself.
- Nothing else. The lake trip happens both nights because the moose drive happens both nights.

| | Night 1 | Night 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **Theme** | Shakedown + core | **Peak meteors + creative** |
| Lake 8:30–11:45 PM | Core, wide + portrait | Core + **silhouettes & light painting** |
| Refractor, evening | **M8/M20** — only chance | porch, acclimating |
| Refractor, 1:00–3:45 | Dark Shark | Dark Shark |
| Cabin 12:30–3:45 AM | Meteors | **Meteors, peak hours** |

**Take the lake trip on the night with the better southern forecast for 9:56–11:30 PM.** If
night 1 looks clear low in the SSW, don't gamble it on night 2.

> **The scope can't be left outside** (theft), so there's no unattended imaging. It lives on the
> **unheated 3-season porch** — assembled, powered, camera cooling — and comes out at 12:15
> already at ambient. **Dew heaters OFF on the porch, ON once it's under open sky.**
> The whole tripod moves assembled so alt/az survives, but the plate can shift — budget a
> **polar align check with a small adjustment** at 12:15.
>
> Which means **night 1 can't be a lake night** — polar alignment needs Polaris at 8:45 and
> you'd be gone by 7:15. Night 1 builds and marks the plate; night 2 goes to the lake.

## Lake kit — load before 7:15 both nights

**PACK THE 6SE** — fork, tripod, eyepieces, 32 mm Plössl. **Leave the EQ wedge home**, it does
nothing for visual. Set the NexStar handset site to **45° 14' N / 71° 12' W** before you go.

**Four separate supplies, one per system — no single point of failure:**

| Load | Power |
|---|---|
| **Canon** | **LP-E17 batteries.** Swap at the **10:45 landscape→portrait break**, not when it dies |
| **Dew strap** | **USB power bank** — ~5 h off 10,000 mAh |
| **6SE** | **Lead-acid direct**, 12 V, no regulator |
| **SAM Mini** | **2×AA lithiums, plus a spare pair** |
| Everything else | Power box, coupler, spare cables ride in the truck as **backup** |

Fuse the alligator lead at the battery end (5 A) and verify centre-positive with a meter before
first use. Full list in [SCHEDULE_1_NIGHT.md](SCHEDULE_1_NIGHT.md#lake-kit).

## Standing notes

- **The 6SE's southern showpieces** — M8, M17, M16, M22, M11 — only exist 9:56 PM to midnight,
  exactly when you're at the lake. **Bring the 6SE along** if your friend wants them; the fork
  travels fine and the lake has the horizon.
- Daylight flats from night 1 cover both nights unless you rotate the camera or change the
  optical train.
- One card per night. Swap, never format in the field.
