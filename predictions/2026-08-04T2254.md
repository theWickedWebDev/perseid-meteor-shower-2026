**7.1 The aerosol reach is four days, not five.** §10 of `FORECAST_FINDINGS.md` says the aerosol

**7.2 "The forecast will sharpen" is contradicted by the spread, not just by the error curve.**

error curve makes, and it is sitting in the current snapshot rather than in a 51-hour sample.

Whether it is a fact about the atmosphere or a fact about using max-minus-min across eight
heterogeneous models as a spread statistic, I cannot tell from here. I lean toward the latter.
An interquartile range across the eight would probably close the gate sometimes, and would be
worth computing alongside — though not instead of, since the extremum is what the tail risk in
